1. **Warm-Black Token Scale (`design-tokens.css:221-224`)**: `--rj-shadow-1..4` use `rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.08/0.04/0.10)` — warm brown-black that clashes slightly with the cool cobalt (`#0D55B8`) and obsidian (`#0B1019`) palette.
