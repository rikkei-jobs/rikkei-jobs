### 7.3. T1a Architectural Choice & Evidence: Portal to `document.body` vs `<dialog>.showModal()`

- **Chosen Approach**: Render `ApplyDialog` and `WithdrawDialog` via **`ReactDOM.createPortal(..., document.body)`** (guarded with `typeof document !== 'undefined'` for SSR safety), NOT native `<dialog>.showModal()`.
- **Empirical Evidence & Technical Justification**:
  1. **`jsdom@31.0.0` Lacks `HTMLDialogElement.prototype.showModal`**: Direct verification in `frontend/` (`node -e` importing `jsdom` `31.0.0`) confirms `typeof d.showModal === 'undefined'` and calling `d.showModal()` throws `TypeError: d.showModal is not a function`. Relying on `showModal()` would break Vitest unless replaced by artificial global polyfills, and `jsdom` still does not emulate the browser Top Layer, `::backdrop`, or `inert` behavior.
  2. **Compatibility with Portaled `Select` Inside Dialogs**: `frontend/src/shared/components/Select.tsx` (line `854`) already mounts `.rj-select-listbox` via `createPortal(..., document.body)`. A `<dialog>.showModal()` modal enters the browser Top Layer and paints over every non-Top-Layer `document.body` portal, obscuring any `Select` dropdown opened inside a dialog. Mounting both dialogs and `Select` in `document.body` keeps them in the same root stacking context so `--rj-z-popover` (`1500`) reliably stacks above `--rj-z-modal` (`1400`).
  3. **Escapes `<main>` Stacking Context & Preserves Full Behavior**: Portal rendering places dialog DOM directly under `document.body`, outside `<main className="rj-dashboard-content">` (`view-transition-name: rj-main-content`) and outside all `transform`, `filter`, `contain`, and `will-change` ancestors, while preserving existing `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`, `handleDialogTabTrap`, `Escape` closing, trigger focus restoration, backdrop click semantics, and `<= 300ms` / `prefers-reduced-motion` animations.
